📖 ZE OFFICIAL BEERFEST RULEBOOK

Read carefully! Ignorance of German law is no excuse for missing a cup!

🏓 1. Yard Pong

Lawn Combat
Setup

Rock / paper / scissors decides who throws first!

Strict Regulations
  • Swatting allowed only after a bucket is hit.
  • NO re-racks allowed. Show grit!
  • Leave the ball in the bucket once sunk.
  • Both balls score = Balls Back!
  • No extra bucket bonuses when scoring into the same bucket.

🎮 2. Beerio-Kart (N64 Championship)

Digital Warfare
Format & Maps

Best of 3, winner takes all! Rock / paper / scissors chooses the first map; the winner chooses all subsequent maps.

The Golden Law
  • NO drinking and driving!
  • Drinks must be 100% finished before crossing the finish line.
  • 1 drink per team per round: when chugging, neither teammate can drive! Kart must be completely stopped.

🥏 3. Kan Jam

Precision Throwing
Setup & Mechanics

First team to exactly 21 wins. Go over 21? You bounce back down to 15!

Scoring Table
  • Dinger (1 pt): Outside hit w/ partner assist
  • Deuce (2 pts): Outside hit unassisted
  • Dunk (3 pts): Inside can w/ or w/o assist
  • DONE (Instant Win): Direct in front slot!

🪵 4. Hammerschlagen (Stump)

Timber Combat
Setup

4 teams per tree stump! Each country team is assigned 1 nail hammered barely into the wood.

The Execution
  • On your turn, you must flip the hammer and follow through in one fell swoop!
  • Drive other teams' nails into the wood.
  • The team with the last nail standing wins!

🍻 5. Flip Cup Arena

Endurance

When your country team is not actively competing in a station match, you must immediately report to the Flip Cup tables! Join up with rotating allies to form fluid "super teams". Click your team's clicker for every win. At the end of initial rotations, top teams triumph!

🏆 TOURNAMENT STRUCTURE & GLORY

Many nations enter. Only one nation lifts the sacred trophy!

1

The Sovereign Nations

Participants are split into teams of 2. Each team represents a proud beer-drinking nation (e.g., USA, Germany, Ireland, Belgium, Mexico, UK, Canada, Czech Republic, Japan, Australia).

2

The 4-Round Rotation & Flip Cup Arena

Every team plays through all 4 station matches (Yard Pong, Beerio-Kart, Kan Jam, and Hammerschlagen). Between matches, dive into the Flip Cup arena and rack up wins using your team's clicker.

3

The Semi-Finals Cut

Once all station rotations conclude, the Flip Cup tallies are finalized. Top 4 teams advance to the Semi-Finals! (The others are respectfully retired to the campfire with cold brews). Semi-finals consist of 1 round of Beerio-Kart, with the map selected by the higher seed.

4

The Grand Finals & The Prize

Semi-final winners clash in the Grand Finals: 1 sudden-death round of Yard Pong! The ultimate champion receives a special prize and a year's worth of bragging rights across Coit Ave!
